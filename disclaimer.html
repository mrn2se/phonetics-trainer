Purpose and Accuracy

Phonetics Trainer is an educational tool intended to help language learners develop an intuition for vowel placement. The formant measurements displayed are approximate and depend on microphone quality, room acoustics, recording duration, and the accuracy of the LPC-based formant tracker. They should not be used for clinical, research, or diagnostic purposes.

Vowel formant targets shipped with the application are derived from published acoustic-phonetics literature. Individual speakers vary considerably; the reference values represent population averages, not prescriptions.

Runtime Audio Content from Wiktionary

When you click Fetch audio from Wiktionary, the application makes a live request to the Wiktionary API and streams an audio file hosted on Wikimedia Commons.

  • Audio files on Wikimedia Commons are contributed by volunteers and are typically released under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 (CC BY-SA 3.0) or a compatible free licence. Each file's individual licence is stated on its Wikimedia Commons page (linked from the badge that appears in the app when audio is loaded).
  • The application does not embed, redistribute, or store any Wiktionary audio. The audio is fetched on demand directly from Wikimedia servers and processed in-memory only.
  • Use of the Wiktionary and Wikimedia Commons APIs is subject to the Wikimedia Foundation Terms of Use.

Third-Party Scientific Data

Vowel formant reference values (F1, F2 for male and female speakers) are based on:

  • Peterson, G. E., & Barney, H. L. (1952). Control methods used in a study of the vowels. Journal of the Acoustical Society of America, 24(2), 175–184.
  • Hillenbrand, J., Getty, L. A., Clark, M. J., & Wheeler, K. (1995). Acoustic characteristics of American English vowels. Journal of the Acoustical Society of America, 97(5), 3099–3111.

Raw scientific measurement data (frequencies, means) is factual information and is not subject to copyright. Attribution is provided here as a courtesy.
