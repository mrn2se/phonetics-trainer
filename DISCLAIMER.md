# Disclaimer

## Purpose and accuracy

Phonetics Trainer is an **educational tool** intended to help language learners
develop an intuition for vowel placement. The formant measurements displayed are
approximate and depend on microphone quality, room acoustics, recording duration,
and the accuracy of the LPC-based formant tracker. They should not be used for
clinical, research, or diagnostic purposes.

Vowel formant targets shipped with the application are derived from published
acoustic-phonetics literature (Peterson & Barney 1952; Hillenbrand et al. 1995).
Individual speakers vary considerably; the reference values represent population
averages, not prescriptions.

## Runtime audio content from Wiktionary

When you click **Fetch audio from Wiktionary**, the application makes a live
request to the [Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/) API and streams an audio
file hosted on [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/).

- Audio files on Wikimedia Commons are contributed by volunteers and are
  typically released under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike
  (CC BY-SA)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) or similar
  free licenses. Each file's individual license is stated on its Wikimedia
  Commons page.
- The application does **not** embed, redistribute, or store any Wiktionary
  audio. The audio is fetched on demand directly from Wikimedia servers and
  processed in-memory only.
- Use of the Wiktionary and Wikimedia Commons APIs is subject to the
  [Wikimedia Foundation Terms of Use](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Terms_of_Use).

## Third-party scientific data

Vowel formant reference values are based on:

- Peterson, G. E., & Barney, H. L. (1952). Control methods used in a study of
  the vowels. *Journal of the Acoustical Society of America*, 24(2), 175–184.
- Hillenbrand, J., Getty, L. A., Clark, M. J., & Wheeler, K. (1995). Acoustic
  characteristics of American English vowels. *Journal of the Acoustical Society
  of America*, 97(5), 3099–3111.

Raw scientific measurement data (frequencies, means) is factual information and
is not subject to copyright. Proper attribution is provided here as a courtesy.
